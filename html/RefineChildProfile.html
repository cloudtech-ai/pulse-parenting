Improve Your Child's Profile with Your AI Assistant

Your child's profile in Pulse Parenting describes their patterns: when hard moments happen, what helps, and what makes things worse. Pulse uses it to tailor its advice when you log a hard moment. You can improve the profile by talking it through with your AI assistant (such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini), and then bring the updated version back into the app.

How the conversation works

  1. Review. The AI assistant shows what your child's profile says now and points out what looks solid, what's thin or missing, and what may be out of date. If the profile is new, it starts from scratch with you.
  2. Improve. The AI assistant asks two or three questions at a time, mostly about specific recent moments. Answer in your own words. Short answers are fine, and you can skip any question.
  3. Finish. When you're happy, say "I'm done, give me the updated profile." The AI assistant summarizes the changes and gives you the updated profile. It won't produce the profile before you ask.

Tips for good answers

  • Describe a real recent moment: what happened just before, what your child said or did, what you tried, and how it ended.
  • Mention what has actually helped, even small things, and what tends to make things worse.
  • Say what's going on right now that might matter, such as a new school, a new sibling, or poor sleep.
  • If you're not sure about something, say so. The AI assistant keeps guesses as open questions rather than facts.
  • Correct the AI assistant whenever it gets something wrong. You decide what goes in the profile.

Bringing the profile back

  • On your phone: tap Copy on the AI assistant's code block, return to Pulse, and tap Paste updated profile.
  • As a file: open the downloaded .json file and choose Open in / Open with → Pulse, or use Import updated profile in the app.
  • Pulse shows every change before saving, and you can accept or skip each one. Saved changes can be undone from the profile's history.

Privacy

Before sharing, Pulse can replace your child's name with "the child", and you can leave out some sections. Your copy goes wherever you send it. Pulse doesn't keep it. Avoid adding details that identify your child, such as their school or town.

Safety

The profile is for everyday patterns. If you're worried about your child's safety or someone else's, contact a professional or your local emergency services.
