Review Your Week with Your AI Assistant

Once a week, Pulse Parenting helps you look back at what happened and choose a few things to focus on next. You can write the review yourself, or talk it through with your AI assistant (such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini). The assistant looks back at the week with you first, then helps you plan a light week ahead. You bring back the review and the plan, and choose what to keep.

The plan is yours: everything on it is something you do, for yourself, for the family, or with one of your children. It's meant to help you be mindful of a few things, not to fill a to-do list.

How the conversation works

  1. Look back. The AI assistant retells your week in a few sentences, starting with what went well: what you planned and did, the good moments you logged, and when things were hard. It reads the episodes you described and says what patterns it notices, as possibilities rather than conclusions. Then it asks a few questions at a time, including how each of your goals went.
  2. Look ahead. It suggests one to three goals for next week, each tied to something from the look-back, and asks about your real schedule. Then it builds a small plan: at most two activities a day, within the time you said you have, with at least one free day. If this week was too full, it plans less, not more.
  3. Finish. When you're ready, say "I'm done, give me the result." The AI assistant tells you what it will send back, then gives you the result. It won't produce the result before you ask.

Activities

  • The AI assistant plans with activities Pulse already has steps for, from the activity library (the same packs you can browse in Pulse under Plan → Discover Routines). It picks ones that fit your children's ages, plus short ones for you and for the whole family.
  • When nothing in the library fits, it may suggest its own activity. Pulse marks those "Suggested by" your AI assistant.
  • If your AI assistant can't open web pages, it will tell you and suggest its own activities instead.

Tips

  • Logging a good moment during the week takes a few seconds, and gives the review something to start from.
  • Say what got in the way of the things you didn't do. Busy weeks are normal, and the next plan should fit them.
  • Keep a goal that's partly working. Small changes take a few weeks.
  • If the AI assistant gets something wrong, say so. You know your family best.
  • You can skip any question, or say you only want the look-back this week.

Bringing the result back

  • On your phone: tap Copy on the AI assistant's code block, return to Pulse, and tap Paste result.
  • As a file: open the result .json file (for example from Google Drive) and choose Open in / Open with → Pulse, or tap Import result on the same screen in Pulse.
  • Pulse shows every part of the result before saving: the look-back, how each goal went, and each goal and activity for next week, with who it's for and on which days. Accept what feels true and ignore the rest. Nothing is saved until you tap Save.
  • If next week already has a plan, you choose whether to add to it or replace it. Anything you've already marked done stays done.
  • Your reviews are listed under Weekly reviews on the Review tab, and each week's review helps the next one.

Profile updates

A week sometimes shows something new about a child. The AI assistant may suggest notes for your child's profile, with a line on why. These are only suggestions: the review never changes a profile. If you want to update it, open the review in Pulse, tap Copy notes, then Update profile, and paste the notes into that conversation.

Privacy

Pulse shares the week being reviewed and the week before it, by date, not time:

  • Your plan, what you marked done, your goals, and (if you choose) the good moments you logged.
  • For each child you include: their age range, a short part of their profile (the summary, what helps, and what makes it worse), their check-ins (the mood only), and the episodes as you described them. Check-in notes, the rest of the profile, and any earlier analysis of an episode stay on your phone.
  • Your own check-ins, only if you turn that on.

Pulse replaces your children's names with "Child A", "Child B" by default, and puts the names back when you bring the result in. Only exact names are replaced, so check your notes for nicknames. Your copy goes wherever you send it. Pulse doesn't keep it.

Safety

If you're worried about your child's safety or someone else's, contact a professional or your local emergency services.
