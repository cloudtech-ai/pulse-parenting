Talk Through an Episode with Your AI Assistant

An episode is a hard moment you logged in Pulse Parenting. You can talk it through with your AI assistant (such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini) to understand what may have been going on and what to try next time. Then you bring back a short analysis for the episode, and any new patterns worth adding to your child's profile.

How the conversation works

  1. Summary. The AI assistant retells what happened in a few sentences and says what may have been going on for your child. These are possibilities, not conclusions.
  2. Talk it through. The AI assistant asks two or three questions at a time: what happened just before, what your child might have been feeling or trying to do, what you tried, and how it ended. It offers a next step to try and a few things you could say.
  3. Finish. When you're ready, say "I'm done, give me the result." The AI assistant tells you what it will send back, then gives you the result. It won't produce the result before you ask.

Tips

  • Add what you remember that isn't in your note, such as what happened earlier that day or how your child seemed before it started.
  • Say what you tried, even if it didn't work. That's often the most useful part.
  • If the AI assistant gets something wrong, say so. You know your child best.
  • You can skip any question.

Bringing the result back

  • On your phone: tap Copy on the AI assistant's code block, return to Pulse, and tap Paste result.
  • As a file: open the result .json file (for example from Google Drive) and choose Open in / Open with → Pulse, or tap Import result on the same screen in Pulse.
  • Pulse shows every part of the result before saving: the analysis for the episode, and each suggested addition to your child's profile. Accept what feels true and ignore the rest. Nothing is saved until you tap Save.
  • The analysis is kept alongside any analysis from Pulse, and you can switch between them. Profile additions are added to the profile without changing what's already there, and can be undone from the profile's history.

Privacy

Pulse shares the episode and a few of your child's usual patterns: the summary, hard moments, what helps, what makes it worse, and current context. Notes and open questions stay on your phone. Pulse can replace your child's name with "the child", and shares the date but not the time. Your copy goes wherever you send it. Pulse doesn't keep it.

Safety

If you're worried about your child's safety or someone else's, contact a professional or your local emergency services.
