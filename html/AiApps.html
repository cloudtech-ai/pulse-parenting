How an AI App Can Help

Pulse Parenting works with the AI chat app you already use, such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Pulse hands it a small file with your child's profile or a hard moment you logged, plus instructions that tell it how to help. You talk it through together, and the app gives back updates that you review in Pulse.

What you can do

  • Build or refine your child's profile. The AI app shows what the profile says, asks you a few questions at a time about recent moments, and gives back an updated profile when you're done.
  • Talk through an episode. The AI app summarizes what happened, helps you understand what may have been going on, and suggests what to try and what to say next time. It gives back a short analysis for the episode and any new patterns for the profile.

How it works

  1. In Pulse, tap Copy (or Share a copy). Pulse copies the profile or episode with its instructions.
  2. Open your AI app, start a new chat, and paste it in.
  3. Answer its questions. When you're finished, say "I'm done."
  4. Copy the AI app's reply, go back to Pulse, and paste it. Pulse shows every change, and you accept or ignore each one. Nothing is saved until you tap Save.

Choosing your app

In Pulse, go to Settings → AI app and pick ChatGPT, Claude, or Other. Pulse uses your choice in its instructions and for the Open button. For another app, you can add its name and web address (for example gemini.google.com); without a web address, you just switch to the app yourself. Any app that can read what you paste and reply with text works.

Privacy

Before sharing, Pulse can replace your child's name with "the child", and some parts of the profile stay on your phone. What you share goes to the AI app you choose, under that app's own privacy terms. Pulse doesn't keep a copy.

Safety

AI apps can be wrong, and they aren't a replacement for professional help. If you're worried about your child's safety or someone else's, contact a professional or your local emergency services.
