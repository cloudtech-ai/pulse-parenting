How an AI Assistant Can Help

Pulse Parenting works with the AI assistant you already use, such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Pulse hands it a small file with your child's profile, a hard moment you logged, or your week, plus instructions that tell it how to help. Think of it as a thoughtful second pair of eyes: you talk it through together, and it gives back updates that you review in Pulse. You decide what's kept.

What you can do

  • Build or refine your child's profile. The AI assistant shows what the profile says, asks you a few questions at a time about recent moments, and gives back an updated profile when you're done.
  • Talk through an episode. The AI assistant summarizes what happened, helps you understand what may have been going on, and suggests what to try and what to say next time. It gives back a short analysis for the episode and any new patterns for the profile.
  • Review your week and plan the next one. The AI assistant looks back at the week with you (what you planned and did, the good moments, and the hard ones), then helps you choose one to three goals and a few small activities for next week. It gives back the review and the plan, and may suggest notes for a child's profile, which you add yourself if you want. More about the weekly review.

How it works

  1. In Pulse, tap Copy (or Share a copy). Pulse copies the profile, episode, or week with its instructions.
  2. Open your AI assistant, start a new chat, and paste it in.
  3. Answer its questions. When you're finished, say "I'm done."
  4. Copy the AI assistant's reply, go back to Pulse, and paste it. Pulse shows every change, and you accept or ignore each one. Nothing is saved until you tap Save.

Choosing your AI assistant

In Pulse, go to Settings → AI assistant and pick yours from the list, such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Pulse uses your choice for its wording and for the Open button. If yours isn't listed, choose Other and add its name and web address (for example copilot.microsoft.com); without a web address, you just switch to it yourself. Any AI assistant that can read what you paste and reply with text works.

Privacy

Before sharing, Pulse can replace your child's name with "the child" (or "Child A", "Child B" in a weekly review), and some parts of the profile stay on your phone. What you share goes to the AI assistant you choose, under that app's own privacy terms. Pulse doesn't keep a copy.

Safety

AI assistants can be wrong, and they aren't a replacement for professional help. If you're worried about your child's safety or someone else's, contact a professional or your local emergency services.
